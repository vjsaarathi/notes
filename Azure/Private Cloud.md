### Private Cloud
+ you own the data center that hosts the environment
+ provide self service access to compute resources to users in your organization 
+ A simulation of a public cloud to users, but you remain completely responsible for maintenance and services that you provide.
+ Users can be external customer or specific internal departments such as accounting or HR
### Advantages
+ Ensure the configuration can support any scenario or legacy application
+ Control (and responsibility) over security
+ meet security, compliance and legal requirements

### Disadvantages
+ Initial investment and purchase of the hardware is on you
+ the startup and maintenance cost is on you
